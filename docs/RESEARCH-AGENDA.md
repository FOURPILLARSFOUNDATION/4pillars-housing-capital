# Housing Capital research agenda

## Near-term outputs

1. A problem brief distinguishing homeowner liquidity, affordability, tenant stability, and ownership concentration.
2. An evidence map of existing state and local programs, their safeguards, and observed outcomes.
3. A model-policy comparison covering eligibility, consumer protections, governance, fiscal exposure, and evaluation metrics.
4. A pilot and sunset design that permits public learning without exposing households to uncontrolled risk.

## Example research questions

- Could a resident-protective shared-equity or liquidity program reduce forced sales without creating a predatory claim on future appreciation?
- What rules prevent institutional buyers from using a public program to concentrate neighborhood ownership?
- Which disclosures, counseling, appraisal, and dispute safeguards are necessary before a household accepts a housing-capital product?

## Evidence standard

Prioritize primary statutes, program reports, public datasets, peer-reviewed research, and clearly labeled assumptions. State distributional effects and implementation risks, not just projected upside.
